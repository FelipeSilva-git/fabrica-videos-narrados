## Description
She looks like she just fell asleep. It has been more than five hundred years.
On top of a volcano, 6,700 meters high, archaeologists found three Inca children. And the hair of the oldest one held a diary of the last year of her life.

In this video you will see the real photos of the 1999 discovery, understand the capacocha ritual, hear a 1621 account of a girl who "asked to die," and find out what science read in every centimeter of that hair.
